WHO WE ARE

More Than Football. We Develop People.

NextGEN Football Academy is a football development organisation committed to identifying, developing and empowering young footballers through quality coaching, structured player development, education, mentorship and international exposure.

We believe football is more than a game. It is a powerful platform for developing discipline, confidence, leadership, teamwork and life skills while creating pathways for young people to pursue opportunities within football and beyond.

Our goal is to create an environment where young players can develop their football ability, understand the demands of the modern game and grow into responsible, confident and well-rounded individuals.

01

OUR VISION

A Future Full of Possibility

To become a leading youth football development platform in Africa, producing technically capable, disciplined and well-rounded players while creating pathways to local and international football opportunities.

02

OUR MISSION

Building Every Player's Pathway

To provide young footballers with access to quality coaching, structured player development, mentorship, education and meaningful football experiences that prepare them for the next stage of their sporting and personal journey.

OUR DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY

We Develop the Complete Player

At NextGEN, we believe development takes time. We do not measure a young player's potential only by goals, trophies or physical ability. We consider the complete player.

01

Technical

Can the player control, pass, dribble, finish and execute skills effectively?

02

Tactical

Can the player understand the game and make good decisions on the pitch?

03

Physical

Is the player developing appropriate physical qualities for their age and stage?

04

Mental

Can the player handle pressure, setbacks and competition?

05

Character

Does the player demonstrate discipline, respect, responsibility and teamwork?
