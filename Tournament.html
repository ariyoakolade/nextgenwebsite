THE STORY

More Than a Tournament

Epaphras Cup 2026 brings young football talents together through competition, player development, teamwork, discipline and excellence. It is a celebration of football and the people and communities that help young players grow.

Hosted in collaboration with Comfort Life Mission International, under the leadership of Pastor Odekunle Epaphras (Olorun Ojiji), the tournament is organised and powered by NextGEN Football Academy.

This edition also marks the launch of Comfort Life FC — a new chapter in the tournament's football and community story.
