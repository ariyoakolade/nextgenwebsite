CAREERS IN FOOTBALL

More Than a Player

Football offers a wide range of professional opportunities beyond becoming a player. Coaching, management, business, media and officiating all help the game grow and connect with people.

01

Player Development

Football coaches can build through practical workshops and ongoing learning, using modern, player-centred methods and strong communication to support technical, tactical and personal development.

  • Professional Footballer
  • Football Coach
  • Performance Analyst
  • Strength & Conditioning Specialist
  • Sports Physiotherapist
02

Football Management

Coordinate the people, operations and events that keep football moving.

  • Team Manager
  • Football Administrator
  • Club Operations
  • Tournament / Event Manager
03

Football Business & Legal

Support the professional, commercial and legal side of the sport.

  • Football Agent / Intermediary
  • Sports Lawyer
  • Legal & Compliance Officer
  • Sports Marketing
  • Sponsorship & Partnerships
04

Media & Officiating

Tell football stories, capture its moments and help matches run fairly.

  • Football Referee / Match Official
  • Sports Journalist
  • Commentator
  • Photographer
  • Videographer
  • Football Content Creator
