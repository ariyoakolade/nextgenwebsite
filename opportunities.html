A WORLD OF POSSIBILITY

More Than a Football Academy

At NextGEN Football Academy, we believe talented young players should have access to opportunities that allow them to develop, compete, learn and experience football in different environments.

Through our network and football development pathways, players may have opportunities to pursue education, experience international football environments and explore pathways in countries with strong football cultures.

Train. Learn. Experience. Progress.

OPPORTUNITY 01 — SPAIN

Study & Play in Spain

Experience the opportunity to combine education with football development in Spain — one of the world's most respected football environments.

Designed for young players who want to continue their education while developing their football abilities in an international environment.

What This Opportunity Can Offer

  • Football development
  • Training in an international environment
  • Educational opportunities
  • Competitive football experience
  • Exposure to Spanish football culture
  • Personal development
  • International experience
